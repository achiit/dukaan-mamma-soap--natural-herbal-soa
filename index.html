About us

Why the name is mamma.

I remember my mother grinding haldi and neem leaves in our kitchen in Kausar Baugh whenever someone in the house had a skin problem. She never bought anything fancy. She trusted what grew around us.

In 2025 I started making soap with the same thinking, and I named it after her. Every bar is made in small batches, so I know what goes into each one.

If you have a question about which soap suits your skin, message me directly. I would rather help you choose properly than sell you the wrong bar.

  • Ingredients you already recognise: neem, tulsi, aloe vera, turmeric, tomato
  • Small batches made locally in Kausar Baugh, Pune
  • Simple packaging, so your money goes into the soap
  • Every soap has its own price, written down openly
  • You talk to the maker himself on WhatsApp or phone
